🛰

The temporal counterpart to OVERWATCH

OVERWATCH is spatial — what surveillance is watching this place, right now. VIGIL is temporal — what has been with you, across time and places. VIGIL reuses OVERWATCH's proven scanning stack and adds a persistent on-device store to reason about a tracker's history.

what it detects

Five ecosystems. One tell: separated from its owner.

VIGIL recognises each BLE wire format and, where the ecosystem signals it, filters to the separated-from-owner state — the only state in which a following tracker is even detectable. VIGIL takes that flag on trust, so a modified tag that always claims its owner is nearby never escalates. Tile has no flag, so every Tile is checked. Chipolo, Pebblebee, eufy and the rest inherit whichever network their SKU joined, so VIGIL detects the network.

🍎

Apple Find My / AirTag

Mfg data, company 0x004C, type 0x12. Separated when the "maintained" status bit is cleared. ~24 h re-link window.

🟢

Google Find My Device

Service data 0xFEAA, frame 0x40/0x41. Frame 0x41 is the cleartext separated state.

🔵

Samsung Galaxy SmartTag

Service data 0xFD5A; the state byte flags lost / overmature-lost — the following state.

🟩

Tile

Service data 0xFEED / 0xFEEC / 0xFE84. No separated signal and a static MAC — always findable, indefinitely.

🔗

DULT (unified, emerging)

Service data 0xFCB2; the near-owner bit (byte 14 LSB) marks separation. The cross-industry standard the whole ecosystem is converging on — VIGIL parses it today.

how it decides

Persistence, not proximity.

A tracker raises an alert only when it clears the co-movement test — the same device, seen at many of your distinct places, over a sustained window. VIGIL judges each tracker's last 24 hours.

📍

≥ N distinct places

Geohash-7 cells; N = 2 / 3 / 4 by sensitivity. A tracker seen only where you dwell isn't following — it lives there.

⏱

≥ 3 sightings over ≥ T minutes

Debounced to one per 15 min; T = 30 / 45 / 90 by sensitivity. Persistence across time, not a single blip.

📶

RSSI proximity gate

Its peak signal must reach −85 dBm at least once at the default setting. AirGuard has no such check, though it's a coarse one: a tag can clear it from tens of metres away. What stops "a Tile in the next car at a few red lights" is the debounce — each tag counts once per 15 minutes, so it never adds up.

OBSERVED

Seen, logged, geotagged. Either never seen separated from its owner (a Tile always counts as separated), or not yet seen three times near enough. No alarm.

SUSPICIOUS

Seen three or more times, 15 minutes apart, with a peak above the RSSI floor, but not yet across enough places or time. No notification yet; VIGIL keeps watching.

ALERTING

Cleared the full co-movement test, so VIGIL raises an alert. "Make it ring" and the hot/cold finder help you find it.

✓
Allowlist — "This is mine"

Tap a tracker to approve it — your own AirTag, your partner's Tile — and VIGIL stops alerting on that identity. Tiles keep one identity; other tags change theirs at least daily and can come back as a new device.

✓
Learned offline baseline

VIGIL learns the places you spend time (home, work) as anchors. A tracker seen at an anchor on three different days, without moving with you, is auto-marked Known (usual places), entirely on-device. In practice that's Tiles, the only tags that keep one identity for days; other household tags stay quiet because they don't travel with you, or report being near their owner.

the hard part

Catching the clone.

Every shipping detector — AirGuard, iOS, Android's built-in — keys on device identity, so a key-rotating clone looks like a stream of one-off devices to all of them. Positive Security's Find You (2022) sends one beacon per key, one every 30 s, from ~2,000 Find My keys. Carried by an iPhone user for five days, it raised no alert on their iPhone.

🎯

Detect the attack, not the device

A rotating clone is one physical radio that stays close and moves with you while its identity churns thousands of times faster than any standards-compliant tracker is allowed to. If it keeps advertising, that is an unbroken, close-range, co-moving RF presence, and VIGIL's PresenceEngine tracks that presence instead of the identity: it counts seamless hand-overs to never-seen identities and gates the alarm on co-movement. In offline replay it confirms a clone that switches keys every 30 s and never pauses in about six minutes. It does not catch everything yet. The published Find You firmware slips past it: one beacon per key, then 30 s of silence, and any silence over 20 s ends the clone detector's run. So does a fast-rotating clone that cycles its radio on and off (30 s on, 30 s off in our tests), and at the default sensitivity, a clone that rotates every ~85 seconds to ~45 minutes (30 min on High, 90 min on Low) slips past both of VIGIL's detectors. Nor does VIGIL watch across days yet: the identity test looks back 24 hours and the clone detector starts fresh each session. Closing those gaps, and testing on real hardware, are what remain.

on-device, private

Fully offline. It never phones home.

🔒

No INTERNET permission at all

There is no server, no account, no telemetry. Every tracker, every sighting, and the entire learned baseline live in an on-device SQLite database. VIGIL never transmits them and cloud backup is off; they leave the phone only if you export an evidence report yourself. Detection listens to what trackers already broadcast and never connects to them.

🔔

Make it ring

Ask a suspected tracker over GATT to play its own sound. AirTags get Apple's own command; Google Find My Device and DULT tags (Chipolo, Pebblebee, eufy, Motorola) get the DULT one, and Apple Find My accessories are supported too. A tag that refuses (as it must while with its owner) shows as refusing. An AirTag never replies, so VIGIL reports its command as accepted, or that it hung up, which an AirTag does after playing its sound.

🌡

Hot / cold finder

A passive proximity meter — warmer/colder from live signal. Works even on silent or modified tags that refuse to ring, which is exactly when you need it.

get it

Sideload in three steps.

1 · download
  • Grab the latest debug-signed APK from Releases.
2 · install
  • Sideload — allow "install unknown apps" for your browser or files app, then open the APK.
3 · grant + scan
  • Grant nearby-devices + location + notifications and keep location on (without a fix VIGIL can't count places, so it can't alert). Pick a sensitivity and tap Start Watching; it keeps watching in the background until you stop it, the phone restarts, or Android closes the app.
